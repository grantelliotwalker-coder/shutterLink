Simulator

Sport Shutter’s side of ShutterLink, running in your browser: the real server/shutterlink-api.js with a pretend database and pretend logins.

    Signed in to Sport Shutter

    This stands in for Sport Shutter’s own login. ShutterLink never sees passwords: every request carries the normal Sport Shutter login, and the server decides whose settings it is from that alone. Switch people and watch the settings page change; sign out and it asks you to sign in.

    Server

    Try the bad days too. The settings page keeps your changes and retries when the server is down, asks what to do when the same settings were saved somewhere else, and shows clear messages for everything else.
